Normalization · option 2

Divide by cell area instead: independent of the panel

value = log( 1 + count / cell area × 1,000 )
A density: molecules per µm². The area comes from segmentation, not from the genes, so the panel choice cannot bias it.
Neuron, 400 µm²
gene X = 10 → 25
Glial cell, 300 µm²

The catch: only as good as the segmentation. A 2D area is also just a proxy for the cell volume.
