Limits

Your clusters can only show what the panel can see

No probe, no cluster
A cell type without markers on the panel merges into its neighbours in expression space. 347 genes cannot separate every type that 20,000 genes can.
Some genes are background
5 of our genes were not above the brightest negative control. Do not build conclusions on them in this sample.
Neighbours leak in
Molecules from neighbouring cells can look like expression, and like differential expression (Cell segmentation lecture).
What if the gene you care about is not on the panel? A matched scRNA-seq reference can predict unmeasured genes and cell types in each spatial cell. That is Lecture 5: integrating single-cell and spatial data.
