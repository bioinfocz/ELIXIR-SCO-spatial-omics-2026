Signs of segmentation trouble in your QC
Very small or very large cell areas: fragments, or merged cells
Unusual nucleus-to-cell area ratio: a nucleus expanded into empty space, or two nuclei in one cell
Markers of two cell types in one cell: molecules from a neighbour counted in the wrong cell
