Roadmap · same order as Practical 2

Five steps from a count table to named cell groups

1
Quality control
Is the run good? Which cells or regions look wrong?
qv, controls, counts, areas, maps
2
Filtering
Remove cells and genes we cannot trust
sc.pp.filter_cells, filter_genes
3
Normalization
Make cells comparable despite size and detection differences
normalize_total + log1p, area, Pearson
4
Reduce & cluster
Group cells with similar expression, then look at them in space
pca, neighbors, leiden, umap
5
Marker genes
Which genes define each cluster? Give it a name.
rank_genes_groups
Steps 2-5 are the scRNA-seq workflow you know. What is new: every step can be checked on the tissue map, and several defaults (cut-offs, normalization, gene selection) need rethinking for a small targeted panel.
Functions as used in Practical 2 (scanpy, squidpy).
