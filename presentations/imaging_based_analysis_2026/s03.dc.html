The measurement

How an RNA molecule becomes a dot with a gene name

1 · Make it visible
A probe binds its target RNA
It is ligated into a circle
Rolling circle amplification makes many copies: one bright spot
2 · Image it round after round

Probes are read out in cycles of hybridisation and imaging. In each round the spot is on in one colour or off.

B = blue, O = orange, · = off. Some codewords are never given to a gene: if they show up, they measure decoding errors.
Each dot gets a quality value (qv): how well its pattern matches the codeword.
