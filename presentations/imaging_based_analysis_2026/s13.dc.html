Spearman correlation of cell area and transcripts per cell
The main trend: a larger outline collects more molecules. Part of this is biology (big neurons have more RNA), part is technical (expansion into neighbours or empty space).
The flat line at the bottom: cells of every size with almost no counts. Before removing them, check where they are on the map (two slides ahead).
Two consequences: cut-offs on counts remove small cell types first, and raw counts must be normalized before cells are compared.
Data: Practical 2 dataset, all 62,268 cells; axes cut at the 99.5th percentile. Practical 2 makes the same plot with plt.scatter.
