Normalization

Why normalize? Same cell type, different counts

Data QC Filter Normalize Cluster Markers
Two neurons of the same type. Cell B was only partly inside the section, or sits in a region with weaker detection.
Raw counts mix two things: how much was captured (technical) and which genes (biology). Normalization tries to remove the first and keep the second.
Without it, clustering groups cells by size and detection, not by type.
