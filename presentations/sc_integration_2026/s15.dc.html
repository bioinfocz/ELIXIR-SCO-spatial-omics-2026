What Tangram maximises (Practical 5 settings)
1 · Gene by gene (main term): for each shared gene, compare its predicted and measured pattern across locations with the cosine similarity (right); average over genes.
2 · Density: cells placed at a location should follow the RNA measured there (density_prior="rna_count_based"); the mismatch (KL divergence) is subtracted.
3 · Location by location: the same score per location. Off by default.
Start from a random M and improve it step by step (gradient descent; 500 rounds in the practical).
