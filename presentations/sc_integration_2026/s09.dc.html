Describe every cell, spatial and reference, by the shared genes only.
2
For a spatial cell, find its k nearest reference cells (SpaGE: k = 50, cosine distance).
3
Its predicted value for an unmeasured gene = weighted average over those neighbours.
The catch: spatial and scRNA-seq data differ technically, so "nearest" in raw shared-gene space can be misleading. SpaGE first aligns the two datasets (next slide).
Abdelaal, Mourragui, Mahfouz & Reinders, Nucleic Acids Res 2020 (k = 50, cosine distance, weighted average). Points: toy illustration.
