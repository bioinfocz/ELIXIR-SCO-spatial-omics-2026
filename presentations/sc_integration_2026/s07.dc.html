Check before you run
Gene names match? Symbols vs Ensembl IDs, duplicated symbols, and upper vs lower case (Tangram lower-cases names: Cux2 → cux2).
Same preprocessing? SpaGE and Tangram take normalized values; cell2location needs raw counts.
Same tissue? Same species, region and, ideally, condition.
